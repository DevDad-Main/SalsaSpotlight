Welcome to SalsaSpotlight

Press Alt + Space (or your hotkey) to open Spotlight search.

Search apps, files, calculations, or type widget names.

Widgets

Right-click any widget to resize it or open its settings.

Drag widgets to rearrange. They snap to the grid.

Press Alt + W to peek/hide all widgets.

Customize

Open Settings from Spotlight to change theme, gap, layout, and more.

Type "reset layout" in Spotlight to re-arrange widgets.
